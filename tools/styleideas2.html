Card Styles 21 – 35

Fifteen techniques, none reusing an approach already in the tools page. Hover each one. 33–35 are three style takes on one real card, the upcoming Flashstone link.

Risograph

Two halftone ink layers printed out of register. They snap into alignment on hover.

Frosted Glass

A genuine backdrop-filter pane over colour blobs that drift behind the glass on hover.

Letterpress

Debossed into the stock rather than glowing off it. Presses deeper on hover.

Ticket Stub

Punched perforation notches cut with a CSS mask, plus a tear-off line.

Isometric Block

A stacked shadow extrusion that compresses as you press it down.

Topographic

Contour rings instead of a square grid. The terrain zooms as you approach.

VHS Tracking

Chromatic split on the title and a tracking band that rolls up the tape.

Die-Cut Sticker

Thick vinyl outline, sitting crooked, with a corner that peels up on hover.

Woven Fabric

A crosshatch weave with a stitched border that tightens toward the edge.

Brushed Chrome

Machined metal fill with a specular highlight that sweeps around the edge.

Marquee Bulbs

A theatre frame of glowing bulbs that blink when you step up to it.

Ruled Index Card

Notepaper with a red margin rule and punched holes down the binding edge.
