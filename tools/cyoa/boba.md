# Choose Your Own Adventure: Boba & Beyond

## Introduction

Daisy, fifteen and perpetually late for bus stops, accidentally spills her boba milk tea on a mysterious street vendor's ancient tea set. She chugs it in a fit of embarrassment, burps once, and passes out. When she wakes up, the world looks different. Her shadow moves independently. She can phase through solid objects for exactly three seconds. Great, right? Except now there are people who know about this power, and they want to use it.

## Fragment 1
Daisy wakes in her Shenzhen bedroom, sunlight streaming through the window like liquid gold. The taste of tea lingers on her tongue, but something is wrong. Her shadow stretches across the floor, then turns around without her moving. She gasps, and her hand phases right through the nightstand. Panic sets in as she realizes her boba was no ordinary drink.

## Fragment 2
The shadow detaches from her feet and slinks toward the window like a living thing. Daisy watches in horror as it begins sketching shapes on the glass—geometric patterns that pulse with faint energy. Her boba was not just tea; it was something ancient, something that woke up inside her. The shadow blinks, then vanishes.

**Go to: 3**, **15 (The neighbor again)**, or **29 (Call someone)**

## Fragment 3
Daisy stands in the middle of her bedroom and tries again. She focuses on a chair, closes her eyes, and counts down from three. One... Two... Three! Her body dissolves like sugar in hot water. She phases through the chair's legs, gasping as she materializes on the other side. It works again. But something cold whispers at the edge of her vision, and her shadow gives a slow, deliberate nod.

**Go to: 4**, **7 (The alleyway)**, or **12 (The neighbor's door)**

## Fragment 4
Daisy practices in secret during lunch at school, hiding behind her backpack while she phases through her desk and the wall behind it. She laughs at herself—this is ridiculous! She could skip detention by phasing through the classroom door. But then a boy across the aisle stares too long, and his eyes flash with that same geometric pattern. He mouths two words: "The Obsidian Circle."

**Go to: 5**, **10 (School cafeteria)**, or **27 (Run home)**

## Fragment 5
That afternoon, Daisy follows him into the school library. He's not a student—he wears a black jacket with silver thread that matches her shadow's patterns. "You drank from the vendor," he says without looking up from his phone. "They're always hungry." Before she can ask what he means, he phases through the bookshelf beside her, leaving only dust and confusion in his wake.

**Go to: 6**, **9 (The library)**, or **13 (Confront him)**

## Fragment 6
Daisy finds herself cornered behind a row of encyclopedias while the boy speaks on his phone. "She's strong," he says. "Phase time is long. We need her before the Festival." The vendor's tea set appears in his hand, wrapped in black silk. Daisy realizes with dawning horror that her boba was stolen intentionally. The vendor knew she'd drink it.

**Go to: 7**, **11 (The alleyway)**, or **25 (Call her)**

## Fragment 7
She escapes through a side door into the humid Shenzhen alley, the boy's voice echoing behind her. "You can't hide forever," he says, phasing through a wall like it's mist. Daisy runs, her heart hammering against her ribs. She passes a boba shop that looks identical to the one from earlier—same vendor, same mysterious set. Something is looping here. Or someone is watching.

**Go to: 8**, **12 (The neighbor)**, or **20 (The festival)**

## Fragment 8
Daisy ducks into an abandoned warehouse near the harbor, breathing hard. The shadows inside seem to stretch longer than they should. She tries to phase through a crate and barely makes it in time before her form flickers back. Her phone buzzes—unknown number: "I see you. Don't fight." She doesn't know who that is, but she feels like this isn't about one person. It's about something bigger.

## Fragment 9

"You're close," he says. "But you don't understand what you've awakened." A woman enters wearing robes that seem to absorb light. "She drank from our ancestor's tea. Now we own her."

## Fragment 10
Daisy tries to phase through the wall, but nothing happens. Panic floods her as she realizes the power is gone—or worse, controlled from somewhere else. The boy smiles and taps his phone. "Control mode engaged." Through a glass panel on the wall, Daisy sees other girls like her, all in similar rooms, all looking terrified. She isn't alone in this.

**Go to: 11 (The alleyway)**, **12 (The neighbor's door)**, or **19 (The underground)**

## Fragment 11
Daisy tries to escape through a side window but finds it locked from the outside. The boy steps forward, his eyes glowing with that same geometric pattern she's seen everywhere. "You can't run forever," he says, and Daisy realizes her shadow is no longer just responding to her—it's obeying him. She has seconds before she's dragged away into the dark.

**Go to: 12 (The neighbor's door)**, **13 (Confront him)**, or **19 (The underground)**

## Fragment 12
Daisy finds herself standing in her neighbor's courtyard, an elderly woman tending to a garden of strange flowers that glow faintly blue. The old woman doesn't look surprised at Daisy's appearance. "You're early," she says, offering a cup of tea from a simple ceramic pot. "But you can leave if you must." When Daisy asks about the boy and the van, the woman sighs. "They think they control this power. They don't know it chose you."

He pauses for a moment, then nods slowly. "We tried to control it before. It didn't work. We need something else." Daisy realizes with dawning horror that he might not be entirely evil—he's desperate, trapped in the same system as everyone else.

## Fragment 17
Daisy rides in the van with the boy and another girl who speaks in riddles. The city lights blur past as they approach a massive structure that looks like a fusion of traditional Chinese architecture and something futuristic. "This is the Festival," the riddle-sayer says. "Where choices echo through time." Daisy feels her shadow twitch, responding to something deep within her. She realizes this isn't just a party—it's a trial.

## Fragment 18
Daisy arrives at the Festival—a sprawling event in an abandoned industrial park that now glows with strange lights. Thousands of people move through halls lined with tea sets, each one pulsing with different colors. The boy leads Daisy to a central stage where she must choose between three pathways. Each represents a different fate: control, freedom, or surrender.

## Fragment 19
She refuses all three options and walks off the stage instead. The crowd gasps as the boy's face falls. "You don't understand," he says, phasing through a pillar. Daisy watches as the Festival collapses into chaos around her. She realizes she can't play their game—this power belongs to her to define, not theirs to control.

## Fragment 20
Daisy watches as the Festival dissolves into a storm of light and shadow. People flee in panic as structures melt into mist. The boy tries to phase through her, but she resists, her form glowing with power she didn't know she had. "You can't fight me," he screams, before phasing back into the crowd. Daisy stands alone in the center of the storm, her shadow standing tall beside her like a loyal guardian.

The boy is gone, the Festival is destroyed, but something remains—a single tea set on the ground, its lid open to reveal a swirling pattern of light. She picks it up, and her shadow reaches out to touch it. It whispers: "The choice was never between them or you. It's about what you build from this."

The Obsidian Circle is a front for something older and more dangerous." She sits on her balcony overlooking Shenzhen's skyline, the city lights twinkling below like scattered stars. Her shadow stretches out beneath her, calm and steady. She thinks about all the choices she's made—refusing control, rejecting surrender, choosing connection instead. The boba was never a curse or a gift; it was an invitation to become something more than what others expected.

## Fragment 29
Daisy's phone rings again—this time from the vendor. "They're looking for you," he says quietly. "But so are we." She looks out over Shenzhen, knowing that her story isn't over. The power she gained is real, and so is the danger waiting in the shadows. But now she understands what it means to carry something powerful—not as a burden, but as a responsibility to those who come after her.

**Go to: 30 (The festival)**

## Fragment 30
She stands at the crossroads of two paths—one leading toward the unknown city center where more tea sets wait, another toward the quiet harbor where new allies have begun gathering. Her shadow stretches out before her, offering both directions equally. The choice is hers alone, and for the first time since she woke up with a superpower, Daisy smiles at what comes next.
