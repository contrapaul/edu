Rules that apply to the whole project

These sit outside any single criterion and are easy to overlook while comparing the three reports. Page references are to the design technology guide.

  • Maximum 35 A4 pages and 3,500 words. The examiner stops reading once either limit is reached. (p. 66)
  • Annotations must be no more than 10 words each. Anything longer counts toward the word count. (p. 66)
  • A4 portrait throughout, except the detailed dimension drawings in Criterion D. (p. 66)
  • A physical fidelity model must be developed for testing and evaluation. Digital modelling does not replace it. (p. 67)
  • Reference sources as footnotes on the page. Footnotes do not count toward the word count, and no separate references page is required. (p. 72)

  • Handwritten annotations must be legible and equivalent to Arial 11pt, with black ink recommended. (p. 66)

Taylor's work sits firmly in the 1–3 band. The primary persona is stated rather than described or explained. We learn only the most basic facts about Sarah (age, occupation, problem) with no research to support the characterisation, no demographic context, and no reference to motivations or behaviour patterns.
