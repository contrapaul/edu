Using AI to learn

A tool that gives you the answer and a tool that helps you learn are the same tool. The difference is what you do with it.

One question, or a conversation

Most people ask a chatbot one question, take the first answer, and leave. What they get is an answer. What they could have got is an understanding.

The branching conversation

A real student prompt, then your choice of what to do next: copy it, ask for an explanation, ask for a source, challenge it, or ask to be quizzed. Each path is a real recorded reply.

Finding sources, and checking them

A model can point you to sources. It can also invent them, or credit a real page with something it does not say. Asking is step one.

The source check

Five citations a model produced. Some are real, some are invented, some are real but say something different. You check each one.

Making it stay learned

Looking something up again is easy, which is exactly why nothing sticks. The habits that make learning last are cheap, and a model can run them for you.
