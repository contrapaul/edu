Ai / Criterion A, Inquiring and analysing

Justify the need for a new game

Statement of inquiry

Designers communicate through the choices they give a player, and those choices decide who the game is really for.

Key concept
Communication
Related concepts
Innovation, Collaboration
Global context
Personal and Cultural Expression
Time
1 class
Worked
Individual
Do1 class
  • Who the audience is, in specifics. Not "Grade 6 students" but what they do at 11:45 on a Tuesday.
  • The situation as it stands, with at least one piece of evidence gathered rather than assumed.
  • Why a tabletop game rather than a club, an app, a sport or nothing at all.
  • What happens if nothing is made. If the honest answer is "nothing much", the need has not been found yet.
Hand in
  • Need Statement, 1 page

Where it goes

Upload to ManageBac as soon as it is finished. If it is not on ManageBac it cannot be marked.

Anything to submit beyond the ManageBac upload has not been decided yet.

“You will choose an audience and find a client group, then conduct research to support the development of a new game.”

This is a student-voice rewrite, not the IB descriptor. Every other strand on the page quotes the descriptor verbatim; Ai is the one that does not. The IB wording for A i is "Explains and justifies the need for a solution to a problem for a client/target audience." Decide which one prints, but the sheet should carry the IB wording verbatim somewhere, the way the other fifteen do.

Bii / Criterion B, Developing ideas

Generate a range of ideas

Statement of inquiry

Designers communicate through the choices they give a player, and those choices decide who the game is really for.

Key concept
Communication
Related concepts
Innovation, Collaboration
Global context
Personal and Cultural Expression
Time
3 classes
Worked
Individual
  1. 1Mechanic draw and rapid concepts
  2. 2Develop and annotate the best six
  3. 3Paper prototypes and the interpretation test
Do3 classes
  • A core loop diagram. What a player does on their turn, in order.
  • Board or component sketch, roughly to scale.
  • Mechanics used, named from the catalog.
  • The meaningful choice, circled and labelled. If it cannot be pointed at, the concept is not finished.
  • Annotation linking to a Bi specification by number.
  • One risk. The part most likely to fail.

On the unit page: The mechanic draw, The interpretation test.

Hand in
  • 6 to 8 concepts + 2 prototypes

Where it goes

Upload to ManageBac as soon as it is finished. If it is not on ManageBac it cannot be marked.

Anything to submit beyond the ManageBac upload has not been decided yet.

Ci / Criterion C, Creating the solution

Plan the build

Statement of inquiry

Designers communicate through the choices they give a player, and those choices decide who the game is really for.

Key concept
Communication
Related concepts
Innovation, Collaboration
Global context
Personal and Cultural Expression
Time
1 class
Worked
Team plan + personal plan
Do1 class
  • Team plan: build a plan for making the game, with time and resources established every step. All members of the group should be able to follow it without asking questions.
  • Personal task plan: Create your own plan for the specific tasks you will be responsible for. It must be detailed enough that other group members can help if you are absent.

On the unit page: The peer follow test.

Key concept

Time
1 class
Worked
Individual
Do1 class
