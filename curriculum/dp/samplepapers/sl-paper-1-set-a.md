**6.** Bambu Lab manufactures desktop 3D printers for consumers and small design studios. The X1 Carbon (Figure 3) has an enclosed heated chamber, a gantry made from carbon-fibre-reinforced polymer, and an automatic material system that holds four filament spools at once.

Bambu Lab also runs MakerWorld, an online platform where users publish and download printable models.

**8.** The Eames Lounge Chair (Figure 5) was designed by Charles and Ray Eames in 1956 and has been manufactured continuously ever since. Chairs made in 1956 are still in daily use. Herman Miller offers the chair in walnut, santos palisander and white ash, with a choice of leather colours, but has not changed its form.

## C. SL scope verification

Content deliberately excluded because it is **HL only**:

- A3.2, A3.3, A3.4 (structural, mechanical and electronic systems). No gear, lever, circuit, force or Young's modulus item appears.
- A4.1 (manufacturing techniques: additive, subtractive, forming, joining, finishing as a classification). Q6a asks only about rapid prototyping technologies, which is A2.2.6 and is SL.
- B3.2, B3.3, B3.4, B4.1 (systems application and selection; production systems).
- C1.3 (beyond usability). **The four-pleasure framework and the ACT model do not appear anywhere in this paper.** Both are used in the HL specimen and both are HL only.

- C4.1 (design for manufacture strategies). No design-for-assembly or design-for-disassembly item appears; Q6e is phrased on C2.2.4 repair.
