## C. SL scope verification

Excluded because it is **HL only**:

- **C4.1 Design for manufacture strategies.** Q6 is a laptop built to be taken apart, which is the most direct invitation to design for disassembly in the whole bank. Every part of Q6 is deliberately written on C2.2 circular economy strategies and C1.1 responsibility instead. Writing 6(a) on "design for disassembly as a DfM strategy" would invalidate the paper.
- **C3.2 Life-cycle analysis.** Q6d asks about waste over a service life and is answered on circular economy strategies, not on life-cycle stages or boundaries.
- **A4.1 and B4.1.** Machining, moulding and manufacture are named as facts in Q6c and Q7, but no part asks the candidate to classify a manufacturing technique or compare a production system. Q6c is material selection, which is B3.1.
- **A3.2, A3.3, A3.4 and B3.2–B3.4.** The Instant Pot is a pressure vessel with a control system and the Dyson is a cyclone, and neither is asked about as a structural, mechanical or electronic system. Q5 is written on safety responsibility, usability and product analysis.
- **C1.3 Beyond usability.** The OXO peeler and the Patagonia advertisement both invite the four-pleasure framework and the ACT model. Q3 is written on C1.2 inclusive design and A1.1 physiology, and Q4 on C2.1 and C1.1.
