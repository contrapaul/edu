**4.** A portable power bank stores energy in rechargeable lithium-ion cells and delivers it to a phone through a USB output.

The cells are rated at 3.7 V. The USB output supplies 5 V, so the bank contains a circuit that raises the voltage, and this conversion is not perfectly efficient.

A protection circuit board sits between the cells and the output. It monitors cell voltage and temperature during both charging and discharging, and disconnects the cells if either passes a set limit.

Airlines limit lithium cells carried in the cabin to 100 Wh per item.
