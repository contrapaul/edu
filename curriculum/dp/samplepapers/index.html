Sample Papers

Until we actually make it to the 'first assessment' in 2027 there are no past papers, and only 4 official sample papers. Past papers might help provide an idea of how students are doing, but the changes were significant and many questions include outdated material.

The papers here have been designed to closely align with the official IB samples. They balance topics against teaching hours, cover all command terms, and as much as possible are formatted like official papers. Question topics were chosen based on my interests, experiences, or preferences, and while they may differ from topics in the official papers they may be somewhat more engaging for students.

In the event you skipped over the above text, these are not official IB papers. I've done my best to produce quality copycat papers, but until we have more official documents I can't guarantee their efficacy.
